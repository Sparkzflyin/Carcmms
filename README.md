# GarageLog

**Maintenance tracking for your cars and motorcycles.** Log your vehicles, open
work orders for what needs fixing, and let maintenance schedules tell you what's
due before something breaks.

**Live:** [carcmms.vercel.app](https://carcmms.vercel.app)

## Features

- **Garage dashboard:** every vehicle at a glance, with open work orders and service status
- **Vehicle profiles:** VIN decode from NHTSA, custom fields, photos, miles, km or engine hours
- **Work orders:** status tracking, priorities, parts and labor costs, checklists, notes and photos
- **Maintenance schedules:** intervals by distance and/or time that open work orders when due, with recommended starter schedules
- **Safety recalls:** open NHTSA recalls for each vehicle
- **Trouble codes:** OBD-II code lookup in plain English
- **Shop:** parts inventory with low-stock alerts, vendors, and reusable job checklists
- **Cost of ownership:** expenses, cost per mile, and spending by month and category
- **Calendar:** due dates, scheduled service and history in one view
- **Service history:** a printable record of every job, ready for a buyer
- **Archive:** searchable history of closed work orders

## Built with

Next.js · React · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS · Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage) · Vercel
